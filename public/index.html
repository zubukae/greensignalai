Executive Summary & Strategic Scope

Bangladesh faces recurring severe hydro-meteorological shocks—including monsoonal flash floods across the Surma-Meghna-Jamuna basins, Bay of Bengal cyclone storm surges, and localized waterlogging. While the Cyclone Preparedness Programme (CPP) and Bangladesh Meteorological Department (BMD) provide macroscopic forecasting, a critical operational gap exists between early forecast issuance, localized sensor verification, and automated last-mile disaster relief dispatch.

GreenSignal AI establishes a unified physical and digital infrastructure connecting grassroots field workers, Upazila operations coordinators, and national ministry executives. While complete nationwide physical deployment across all remote channels is a multi-decade endeavor, the core GreenSignal AI software platform is engineered to be fully operational and connecting to existing meteorological and hydrological data within 1 to 2 months.

Multi-Tier Role Applications

Tailored client experiences for Field Responders (low-bandwidth/offline-first PWA & mobile app), Upazila/District Command Coordinators (tactical GIS and relief allocation suite), and Ministry Personnel (macro analytics, resource rebalancing, and national siren broadcast).

Physical Infrastructure

Upazila operations hubs and climate-hardened Union relief warehouses and buffer silos equipped with solar-powered cold-chain medical storage and airtight grain preservation.

Automated Environmental Sensor Network

Automated IoT telemetry stations across river basins, coastal surge points, and sluices providing 24/7 autonomous ground truth without relying on human observer presence during severe weather.

Integration & Modernization

Ingests existing telemetry from BMD Doppler radars, FFWC hydrologic gauges, and SPARRSO satellite data, modernizing manual relief distribution into biometric and QR-code verified workflows.

Software Architecture & Live Codebase Walkthrough

The GreenSignal AI digital platform is built on an enterprise-grade TypeScript/Node.js microservices framework paired with PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, and Socket.io real-time event streaming:

CLIENT LAYER PWA / Android

Field Worker Application

  • Offline-first execution utilizing client-side caching (IndexedDB/Service Workers) ensuring full operational continuity under intermittent 2G/EDGE connectivity.
  • GPS-tagged emergency distress reporting and survivor evacuation routing.
  • QR-code verified relief handoffs eliminating duplicate ration distribution.
COMMAND SUITE public/platform.html

Coordinator Tactical Suite & GIS

  • Interactive Leaflet GIS mapping rendering nationwide upazila disaster nodes, relief shelters, and live water-level telemetry.
  • Relief allocation optimization engine calculating food grain, potable water, medical supplies, and temporary shelter kits against real-time population density.
  • District-level bounding zoom and automated resource shortfall flagging.
REAL-TIME LAYER src/services/messaging.ts

Privacy-Isolated Real-Time Messenger

  • JWT-authenticated Socket.io rooms separate the shared responder channel, private direct conversations, and disaster incident groups scoped to Bangladesh administrative areas.
  • Browser-side AES-GCM message encryption, RSA-OAEP key wrapping, and RSA-PSS sender signatures; the server stores ciphertext and a recovery-key-encrypted private-key backup.
  • Debounced responder directory search, explicit group membership, and persisted incident, location, and message records.
DATA LAYER prisma/schema.prisma

Relational Data Model & PostgreSQL

  • Normalized schemas managing DistrictNode, User, ReliefCenter, EnvironmentalSensor, DisasterAlert, ShelterAllocation, and ChatMessage.
  • Foreign key cascades and indexes enabling sub-millisecond lookups under crisis traffic.

Network Infrastructure for Multi-Million User Scale

To guarantee 99% uptime across all emergency operations hubs during severe cyclone landfalls and national flood crises:

Tier-IV National Data Center Hosting

Primary deployment hosted in the Bangladesh Computer Council (BCC) Tier-IV National Data Center (Kaliakoir, Gazipur) featuring dual 2N redundant electrical feeds, seismic isolation dampers, and active-active disaster recovery failover.

Redundancy: 2N Hot Standby Cluster

Global Edge & BDIX Anycast Routing

Edge layer terminating SSL, filtering DDoS attacks (up to 500 Gbps), and serving static web assets directly through the Bangladesh Internet Exchange (BDIX) domestic peering fabric for ultra-low sub-15ms latency across all divisions.

Latency: < 15ms Domestic Routing

Kubernetes Autoscaling & In-Memory Cache Grid

Microservice compute pods dynamically scale instances on demand via Horizontal Pod Autoscalers (HPA). A distributed Redis Enterprise Cluster powers real-time pub/sub event broadcasting, sustaining millions of persistent connections without packet drops.

Capacity: High-Volume Concurrent WebSockets

Time-Series Telemetry Ingestion (TimescaleDB & PgBouncer)

High-velocity PostgreSQL hyper-tables continuously ingest sensor telemetry readings from river radar gauges, rain sensors, and piezometers with real-time anomaly detection and automated 7-day downsampled rollups.

Throughput: Continuous High-Velocity Sensor Ingestion

Starlink Satellite Integration & 99% Uptime Command Hubs

Dedicated private APN (Access Point Name) with zero-rated responder data across mobile network operators. Each command center will be equipped with Starlink satellite terminals to guarantee 99% uptime during severe cyclonic landfalls, terrestrial fiber cuts, and cellular tower blackouts, backed by solar-powered LoRaWAN mesh gateways.

Connectivity: Starlink Satellite Backup • Guaranteed 99% Uptime

Implementation Roadmap (Rapid 1–2 Month Launch & Multi-Decade Scaling)

While complete nationwide physical civil works and river basin sensor saturation is a multi-decade national endeavor, the core GreenSignal AI digital platform is architected to be fully operational and connecting to existing data streams within 1 to 2 months.

PHASE 1 (MONTHS 01–02) Target Milestone: Rapid Core Launch & Data Integration

Cloud Core, Live Data Ingestion & Command Center Operations

Deployment of the core digital platform on Tier-IV cloud infrastructure, live API ingestion of existing BMD radar and FFWC hydrologic gauge data, activation of Upazila operations hubs with Starlink connectivity for 99% uptime, and field responder mobile PWA availability.

PHASE 2 (MEDIUM-TERM EXPANSION) Target Scope: Regional Hub Retrofits & Sensor Expansion

Regional Hub Expansion & Sensor Grid Rollout

Progressive rollout of Upazila operations hubs, construction of flood-proof Union relief buffer warehouses and silos, installation of dedicated radar and rain gauge telemetry stations, and onboarding of grassroots responder networks.

PHASE 3 (LONG-TERM MULTI-DECADE HORIZON) Target Scope: Complete Nationwide Delta Saturation

Nationwide Saturation & Inter-Agency Command Integration

Continuous decadal scaling of high-density IoT telemetry across all delta river corridors and coastal polders, AI predictive hydro-meteorological model refinements, Starlink satellite network resilience, and multi-agency coordination with Armed Forces and emergency services.
